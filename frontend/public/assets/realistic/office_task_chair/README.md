# Task-chair candidates

The accepted candidate is `nisu/Office_Chair.fbx` with its five PBR texture
maps. It is a CC0 upload with an ergonomic office-chair silhouette (high back,
armrests, gas lift and five-star caster base). Load the FBX and resolve texture
paths relative to `nisu/`; see `nisu/manifest.json` for checksums and provenance.

The alternative OpenGameArt `plaggy` upload was inspected but is not bundled:
it is an older untextured OBJ/DAE model with AO/sculpty maps and is less suited
to the requested modern ergonomic-office look.
